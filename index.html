FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is this safe for my Facebook account? ⌄
Amplr uses human-like delays, randomized jitter, and smart posting strategies to mimic natural behavior. However, like any automation tool, use it responsibly and within Facebook's group posting norms.
Do I need to give you my Facebook password? ⌄
Never. Amplr is a Chrome extension that runs in your browser using your active Facebook session. No credentials are stored or transmitted anywhere.
How does spintax work? ⌄
Wrap alternate phrases in curly braces separated by pipes: {Hello|Hi|Hey}. Amplr randomly picks one variation per group, so every post looks unique.
Can I schedule posts for specific days? ⌄
Yes. Pick any combination of days (Mon/Wed/Fri, weekdays only, weekends, etc.) and set a time. Posts will be delivered on schedule automatically.
Is it really free? ⌄
Yes. The free tier includes unlimited scheduled posts, up to 50 groups, spintax, templates, and analytics. Pro adds multi-account, AI generation, and unlimited groups.
