FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is this safe for my Facebook account? ⌄
Reachr gives you cooldowns, randomized timing, and group limits so you can post in a controlled way. Like any automation tool, use it within reason and follow the rules of the groups you post in.
Does Reachr store my Facebook password? ⌄
Never. Reachr works through your own Chrome browser and active Facebook session. Your Facebook password is not stored or sent anywhere.
What is AI paraphrasing and do I need my own AI account? ⌄
AI paraphrasing rewrites the same core message into different versions for each group. If enabled, choose your AI service in Profiles and keep the rewrite prompt natural.
What happens if my computer is off when a post is scheduled? ⌄
Reachr keeps scheduled posts ready. As soon as Chrome is open again and you are signed in, Reachr catches up on anything it missed.
How many groups can I post to? ⌄
As many as you're accepted into. Reachr imports your Facebook groups with one click and skips groups where posting is not available.
