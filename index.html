What the pilot is — and isn't

Our side: campaign planning, group review, tailored drafts, setup, and monitoring. Your side: your offer and brand context, access to an eligible Facebook presence, and approval of the plan. We agree on the exact scope together; this isn't a promise of a fixed number of posts, leads, or results.

The service

A campaign run with you, not handed to you.

We bring the people and the operating system. You bring your business context and make the final call on what represents your brand.

Message & content

We turn your offer into drafts suited to the groups and audiences we agree to approach. You review the voice and call to action.

Group & campaign planning

We review relevant groups, their rules and the posting identity, then propose a manageable schedule rather than a volume target.

Execution & visibility

We set up the approved workflow, monitor posting outcomes, and share the live post links and any issues worth discussing.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is this software I have to run myself? ⌄
Not for this pilot. We help design and manage the agreed workflow. Reachr is the tool behind it, and we will discuss any setup or access needed before starting.
Will you need my Facebook password? ⌄
No. Reachr uses an active Chrome/Facebook session rather than collecting your Facebook password. We agree on the posting identity and setup together.
Who approves posts and groups? ⌄
You approve the campaign approach, message, groups, identity, and timing before it runs. We also check group rules and skip groups that are not appropriate or where the account lacks access.
What does the pilot cost and include? ⌄
We scope that in a conversation based on your offer, groups, and setup. There is no published fixed package or price on this page.
Can you guarantee posts or leads? ⌄
No. Group permissions, moderation, account access, and audience response are outside our control. We keep the pilot measured and report actual outcomes rather than treating a queued post as a result.
