Beta privacy summary

Privacy Policy

Reachr is a Chrome-based Facebook group posting dashboard. This page explains the practical privacy boundaries while the product is in beta.

No Facebook password storageReachr does not ask for, store, or transmit your Facebook password.
Your browser session is usedPosting actions run through your own Chrome browser and active Facebook session.
You control campaignsYou choose the groups, copy, schedule, first comment, cooldowns, and limits.
Beta productFinal legal language may be updated before paid access or Chrome Web Store launch.

Information Reachr may store

  • Your Reachr account email and authentication records.
  • Facebook group names/URLs you import into the dashboard.
  • Draft posts, scheduled posts, campaign settings, tags, cooldowns, and posting history.
  • Optional AI rewrite settings or prompts you configure.

Facebook credentials

Reachr does not store your Facebook password. The product is designed to work from your active Chrome session, so you stay logged in directly with Facebook in your own browser.

AI rewriting

If you enable AI paraphrasing, the post text you provide may be sent to the configured AI provider to create rewritten versions. Do not put sensitive personal information in prompts or post drafts.

Responsible use

Reachr includes guardrails such as cooldowns, group limits, rest days, and stop conditions. You are responsible for following Facebook rules, group rules, and applicable laws.

Deletion or questions

Contact the Reachr operator to request account/data deletion or privacy clarification while the product is in beta.
