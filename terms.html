Terms of Service

Reachr is currently a beta tool for organizing and scheduling Facebook group posting from your own browser session.

Use Reachr responsibly. Automation can affect account trust if abused. Follow Facebook rules, group rules, and applicable laws.

Beta access

Reachr is offered during beta for testing and feedback. Features, limits, availability, and pricing may change before public launch.

Your responsibility

  • You are responsible for the posts, links, claims, and comments you publish.
  • You must follow Facebook's terms, group rules, and applicable marketing/spam laws.
  • You should use cooldowns, rest days, group limits, and stop conditions instead of blasting every group at once.

Account access

Reachr does not store your Facebook password. Posting depends on your own Chrome browser session and the extension connection.

No guarantee of results

Reachr helps organize posting workflows, but it does not guarantee reach, approvals, engagement, sales, or that every group will allow a post.

Acceptable use

Do not use Reachr for illegal content, deception, harassment, malware, phishing, spam, or activity that violates platform or group rules.

Updates

Final legal terms may be updated before paid access or Chrome Web Store launch.
